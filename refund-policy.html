Refund & Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placing the order.

If the order has already been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.

Refunds will be processed only after the cancellation request has been reviewed and approved.

Refunds for prepaid orders will be credited back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds (if applicable) will be processed via bank transfer after confirmation.

To request a cancellation or refund, please contact us at support@aixaclothing.com with your order details.

AIXA Clothing reserves the right to refuse refund requests if misuse or fraud is suspected.
